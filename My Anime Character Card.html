About my character

Character name: Kamado Tanjiro.

Best friends: Agatsuma Zenitsu, Hashibira Inosuke.

Favourite food: Taranome.

Breathing techniques: Sun breathing and Water breathing.

Why he is my favourite character?

He is one of my favourite character because he never gives up, even though he lost his entire family and his beloved sister turned into a demon, he didn't give up, he used it to become stronger. He is also very kind, even to demons. He fights fiercely but stops when he realises the demon lost, he kindly hears its last words and respect it. He even feels sad about its backstory and prays for it to have a peaceful death and he never shows hatred for anyone even the ones who hates him the most.
