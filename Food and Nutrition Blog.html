Why a Balanced Meal Matters?

A Healthy Plate is important because it provides all the nutrients our body needs in sufficient quantities. It contains different nutrients like protein, fat, cabohydrate, vitamins and minerals in proper quantities. Without these our body won't be able to function properly.

Nutrition tips for students

Students can't always be caretaking their body, so it's better to follow some important tips.

Stay Hydrated

Always being hydrated is important because our body needs water to regulate our body temperature and many more. So it's always best to carry 1-2 litres of water in a waterbottle or some homemade juice especially if you've p.e class.

Make your food colorful

If you are the one who hates eating food, make your lunch and snack box colorful and interesting. You can include some vegetable salad, rice with your favourite curry as your lunch and some nuts, grains and fruits as your snack.It is healthy and also capture your eyes and make your taste buds crave for more.
